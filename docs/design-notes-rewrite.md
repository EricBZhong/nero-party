# Design Notes from Mockup Review

## 01-focus-room.png
- Dark background with concert-like atmosphere (crowd silhouette at bottom)
- Massive song title text centered ("FEEL SOMETHING") - very large, bold
- Artist name below in medium weight
- Green waveform progress bar with timestamps (2:34 / 4:09)
- Two large pill buttons: "Save" and "Top 3" side by side
- Left sidebar: "UP NEXT" queue with numbered tracks (album art + title + artist + "Added by X")
- "See full queue" button at bottom of sidebar
- Top nav: "Nero Party" logo left, "LIVE" indicator + "Focus Listening Room" center, participant avatars + count right
- "Your Top 3" section below main actions with 3 empty slots (+ Add track)
- Overall: stage-first, current song dominates, queue is a compact left rail

## 03-add-song-modal.png
- Centered modal overlay on darkened room background
- "Add a song" title at top
- Two tabs: "Audius search" (active) and "Upload a track"
- Search input with search icon button
- Drag-and-drop zone for audio files
- Title and Artist input fields
- Queue position indicator (#4)
- Source policy callout: "Nero Party is a source-first listening room. Add music you own or have permission to share."
- Large green "Add to queue" CTA button
- Bottom bar shows now-playing track info

## 04-top3-drawer.png
- Right-side drawer panel
- "Your Top 3" title with "PRIVATE RANKING" badge
- "Use the arrows to reorder your Top 3. Only you can see this."
- Three large ranked cards with album art, title, artist, and explicit move controls
- #1 has "NOW PLAYING" badge
- "LISTENED HISTORY" section below with track list (art + title + artist + duration)
- "Save as Top 3" button at bottom
- Explanation: "This saves your current ranking as your Top 3. It won't change the room's queue."
- Left side still shows now-playing info

## 05-final-reveal.png
- Full-screen celebration scene with green spotlights/rays
- "NERO PARTY" kicker, "WE HAVE A WINNER!" massive headline
- Podium layout: 1st place center (elevated), 2nd left, 3rd right
- Each podium has glass-like card with album art, title, artist, points
- Crown icon on #1
- "HOW POINTS WORK" card on right: 5/3/1 explanation
- Participant reactions on left side (avatars + messages)
- More reactions on right side
- Bottom: "PLAY AGAIN" and "EXPORT PLAYLIST" buttons
- Very theatrical, event-like energy

## 08-create-join.png
- Split layout: form left, live preview right
- Left: Party Title input, Mode selection (Focus/Companion), Source Policy, Max Submissions, Invite Code, Discord Voice toggle
- Two CTAs: "CREATE PARTY" and "JOIN WITH CODE"
- Right: atmospheric room photo with Nero neon sign, green lighting
- "LIVE PREVIEW" indicator top-right
- Bottom stats bar: Room Capacity, Current Vibe, Queue Type
- Overall: too dashboard-y per design notes, should be simplified

## Key Design Principles
- Stage-first: current song is the room
- Dark, atmospheric, concert-like mood
- Green (#31f176) as the live accent throughout
- Large confident typography for song titles
- Queue as a supporting rail, not the main structure
- Modals/drawers for secondary flows (add song, ranking)
- Finale as a theatrical reveal moment
- Minimal chrome, no dashboard feel
